:::box[The pitch]{icon="sparkles" span="2"}
**Worm Battlegrounds** is a turn-based artillery game on Roblox that mixes three games:

- **Worms:** a team of small fighters, destructible terrain, and a big, silly arsenal.
- **GunBound:** angle and power aiming with wind, and characters (mobiles) that each play differently.
- **Roworld:** the lobby: play and join matches in the middle, party and loadout on the left, crates on the right.

The game is just starting. The pages fill in as we design it together.
