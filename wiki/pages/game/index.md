Worm Battlegrounds is a turn-based artillery game: teams of worms take turns aiming weapons across destructible maps, and
the last team standing wins. Aiming and wind come from GunBound, the arsenal and the chaos from Worms, and the lobby
from Roworld, drawn in our own style. It plays on PC, phone, tablet and console.

:::stats
- Weapons at launch: 13
- Utilities at launch: 6
- Maps at launch: 5 + 1 HUGE
- Turn: 15 s
- Players per match: 2–8
:::

## Look and sound

The game is **2D**, like Worms: everything happens on one side-on plane, with the camera locked to the side. It's
built in Roblox in the classic **studded brick** style: maps, worms and props look like they're made of studded
Roblox parts, and explosions knock chunks out of the brick terrain.

The sound is **cartoony**: punchy explosions and splats, and worms with **silly voices** that shout and taunt,
like in Worms. The lobby music is a **cartoon-style military march**.
