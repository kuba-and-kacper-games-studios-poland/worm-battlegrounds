Emotes are short animations with a silly voice line. They're **bought with coins**, never from a crate: the player
always knows what they get. The better ones need a **higher level** before they can be bought, up to the max level,
100 (see [[Levels & XP]]).

## The 9 emotes

| Emote | Level needed | Coins |
|---|---|---|
| **Wave** | — | Free, from the start |
| **Thumbs up** | — | 100 |
| **Laugh** | — | 250 |
| **Dance** | 10 | 500 |
| **Flex** | 20 | 900 |
| **Taunt** ("come at me") | 35 | 1,400 |
| **Cry** | 50 | 1,900 |
| **Backflip** | 75 | 2,400 |
| **Victory Trumpet** | **100** | **3,000** |

All 8 bought emotes together cost 10,450 coins.

The names, levels and prices are a first proposal.

## Using emotes

- **In the lobby:** a **smiley button above the loadout** (left side) expands into a row of the emotes the player
  owns.
- **In a match:** the same smiley, above the loadout bar, opens an **emote wheel**: a circle of the player's
  emotes to pick from.
- In a match, the worm plays it where it stands, with its voice line. One emote every **5 seconds**, so nobody
  spams.
- Emotes work during anyone's turn and while spectating.
