**What changed.** The owner asked for the wind to run from −20 to 20, with 20 as strong as 10 was ("so 10 becomes 20 and
the strength is more spread out"), mostly −5 to 5, and lasting 2 turns for everyone.

- **Scale.** The wind goes from −20 to 20 (was ±10). Match's `WIND_MAX` is 20.
- **Push.** It's the same at twice the number: `wind × (WIND_ACCEL + WIND_ACCEL_GROWTH × |wind|)`, with
  `WIND_ACCEL` 3.5 (was 7) and `WIND_ACCEL_GROWTH` 0.375 (was 1.5). That gives 8.5 studs/s² at 2, 34.5 at 6, 96 at 12
  and 220 at 20, the old figures for 1, 3, 6 and 10.
- **Steep shots.** At 12 a shot steeper than about 64° into the wind is blown back; at 20, steeper than about 42°.
- **How often.** `WIND_WEIGHTS` holds 21 weights for strengths 0 to 20, out of 100. About 65% of rolls are −5 to 5,
  ±10 or stronger about 12%, and ±20 about 0.3%. Each side has even odds.
- **How long.** A wind blows for `WIND_ROUNDS` (2) rounds, so every snail plays two turns on it. Match rolls a new one
  on the first turn of every second round, never the same as the last one. Before this, it changed every turn.
- **Particles.** Atmosphere's per-point pull on drifting particles, `WIND_PUSH`, is 0.175 (was 0.35), so the ambient
  particles still look the same.
- **Bots.** They read the wind's push, not its number, so they play as before.

**Where.** `WIND_MAX`, `WIND_ROUNDS`, `WIND_WEIGHTS` in Match's Settings, and `Turns._rollWind`. Combat's
`WIND_ACCEL`, `WIND_ACCEL_GROWTH` (`I.G_Combat.GetWindAcceleration`). Atmosphere's `WIND_PUSH`. The HUD shows the
number (`+12`), as before.

**How to check.** In a match, the wind number stays the same for two rounds, then changes. A ±20 wind blows a 45°
Bazooka shot as ±10 did before.

**For the wiki.** Wind: the −20 to 20 scale, the odds, two rounds per wind, and the push table at the new numbers.

Supersedes 2026-11-15-wind-every-turn (the wind now changes every second round) and 2026-11-15-wind-weights (the new
weights). The tutorial's own wind is in 2026-11-15-tutorial-full-match.
