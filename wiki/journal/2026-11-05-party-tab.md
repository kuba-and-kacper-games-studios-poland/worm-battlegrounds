**What changed.** The Party panel showed no members (its list got no height). It now lists every member and grows with
the party, up to 4. Above it sits a tab, "PARTY 2/4" (members out of `MAX_PARTY`); pressing it opens or folds the
panel. It starts open on PC and console, and folded on phones and tablets, where the open panel covers the Loadout
card. The tab is blue with the arrow up while open, slate with the arrow down while folded.

**Where.** Party gui: `U_Party` frame › `S_Tab` (Button, `Tab` style) and `S_Panel` (Box, `Panel`); Mobile scale 0.85.
Party `Client.lua`: `_onPlatformChanged` (open unless `I.Platform.Current` is `Mobile`), `_refreshTab`, the count in
`_refresh`. Loadout and MatchBoard export at `DisplayOrder` -1 so the opened panel draws over them.

**How to check.** In the lobby on PC the tab reads "PARTY 1/4" with the panel open; a click folds it. In Studio's phone
emulator it starts folded.

**For the wiki.** Party: the tab and its count, open on PC, folded on phones; the member list (avatar, name, level,
crown, Kick).
