**What changed.** The guis' placeholder look (flat purple panels, Fredoka One) is replaced by a Worms-style look made
chunkier for Roblox. Two earlier looks (Dark refined, Brick toy) were rejected and are gone from the guis.

- Flat teal panels with a light bevel line inside a thick navy outline. Nothing is raised: no darker edge under a
  button, tile or panel, and no drop shadow.
- Every window has a dark striped header bar with its title left-aligned in heavy upper-case Gotham, and a red X.
- Boxes inside a window have a smaller striped title band.
- Items (weapons, crate results, diamond packs, bot difficulties) are tiles: the name on a white strip, the picture in
  a sky-blue well, the rarity in the outline colour.
- Buttons are flat teal with outlined capitals; the main action is yellow with navy text; the selected tab or option
  is light aqua with navy text; Leave, Kick and close are red.
- A first icon kit drawn for the game: the seven weapons (bazooka, grenade, shotgun, jetpack, airstrike, cluster
  bomb, black hole grenade), coin, diamond, crown, smiley, a crate placeholder, arrows, a plus and the header stripes.
  They are local previews, not uploaded to Roblox yet.

The Shop's VIP benefits line is four lines tall. All 22 guis are exported into the place: 17 ScreenGuis and the 3
billboards (`DamagePop`, `DropMarker`, `WormTag`) in StarterGui, `LobbyGui` and `TeleportGui` in
`ServerStorage.Ume.UtilityGuis`. The game's `Currency` and `LobbyGui` replace the template's.

**Where.** The theme's `Tokens.lua` (palette, Gotham, sizes), `Styles/` and `Controls/`: `Slab` (the flat face, its
`Bevel` and `Pattern` keys), `Box` (the title band), `Widget` (the header bar), `Item` (strip and well), `Button` and
`Label` (`Upper`). The icons are `Icons.lua` (`wb-*` names, made by the kit in the game home's
`assets/_inbox/ui_kit/`); the guis name them (`wb-coin`, `wb-diamond`, `wb-crown`, `wb-smiley`, `wb-bazooka`…), and
the previews pick a weapon's icon from its name. The Shop change is the `Paragraph` style on `S_Benefits`.

**How to check.** Build any gui in `src/guis/WormBattlegrounds/` in the Gui tool: teal panels, striped headers, no
edge or shadow under anything. `Icons.lua` lines point at `rbxasset://textures/wb_guipreview/…` until the upload.

**For the wiki.** Guis: describe the look (colours, Gotham, the header bars, the tiles, the flat buttons) in place of
the placeholder look the scaffold entry (`2026-11-03-gui-scaffold.md`) describes. Still unsure: the icon kit waits for
the owner's approval, then upload; each icon's asset id then replaces its local path, and the wiki can list them.
