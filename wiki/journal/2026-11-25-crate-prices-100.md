**What changed.** The owner: "make the crates more expensive - 100 gems per crate (so 3 - 300, 10 - 1000)". The
Weapon, Utility and Skin crates cost 100 diamonds for one, 300 for three and 1,000 for ten (were 50, 150, 500). The
hidden Premium and Tutorial crates keep their prices.

**Where.** Each crate row's `Prices.Diamonds` (`One`, `Three`, `Ten`) in `Crates/Data/Crates/<Crate>.lua`; the
server charges through `I.G_Crates.GetPrice`. Crates' README table and practices.md's crate line updated.

**How to check.** In the lobby open WEAPONS (or SKINS): the x1, x3 and x10 buttons read 100, 300 and 1,000.

**For the wiki.** Crates: the price table and a History line. Any page that says how many matches a crate takes.
