**What changed.** Four maps show a custom painted sky behind them instead of the shared stock sky, calm so the map
stays the focus (the owner: "just a background, so players focus on the main level"):

- Picnic Plains: spring morning, soft blue with round cartoon clouds, far green hills and pale peaks.
- Volcano Isle: ember dusk, a plum sky over an amber horizon, two far volcanoes with glowing craters and smoke at the
  sides, clouds lit orange from below.
- Rockslide Ridge: dusty highland, a pale overcast with long thin cloud bands and layered jagged ridges. The map is an
  enclosed cave under a dark haze, so this sky shows only faintly past its side walls and over its roof.
- Desert: golden afternoon, a clear teal sky with a few wisps and two small clouds, faded mesas low on the horizon.

Since later that day Echo Caverns, now a snowy mountain, has a fifth: Mountains, a crisp alpine morning with far
snowy peaks toward the sides; Rolling Plains wears Picnic Plains' (entry `2026-11-25-three-map-rotation`).

Sun and moon are hidden on these maps. Every other map (Mirage Mesa, Lakeside Showdown, Fluffy Fallout, Training Meadow)
and the lobby keep the sky they had.

**Changed later that day.** In a match the skies barely showed (the owner: "the bottom half is too long… the top
part where actual skybox is is barely visible"): the match camera's field of view is only 30°, and an Atmosphere
paints everything under the horizon in its own colour. All five were repainted with their horizon about 10° under
eye level and the blue starting just over it, and a map with its own sky now turns the Atmosphere's fog off
(`MAP_SKY_ATMOSPHERE`: density, haze and glare 0, offset 1), so the sky shows all the way down behind the map. New image ids in
`art/map_skies/README.md`; the first set is unused.

**Where.** Atmosphere's `PROFILES` rows gained `Sky` (the six Sky face image ids and `CelestialBodiesShown` false);
Mood's `_refreshSky` puts it on as a lighting layer (`MAP_SKY_WEIGHT` 2, `MAP_SKY_ID` "MapSky") over the map's preset
and takes it off on a map without one. The faces are drawn by `art/map_skies/skies.py`; their decal and image ids are
in `art/map_skies/README.md` (uploaded to The Snail Studio group, all approved by moderation).

**How to check.** Play a match on Picnic Plains: Lighting's Sky `SkyboxFt` is `rbxassetid://79872244283439` and
`CelestialBodiesShown` is false (checked in a Studio playtest, 2026-11-25). The other three were checked in Studio
edit mode with their presets applied, not yet in a played match.

**For the wiki.** Each of the four map pages: a line on its sky in the look or atmosphere section, and a History line.
The Maps page, if it describes the look: these four have their own skies.
