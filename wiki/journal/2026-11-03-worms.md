**What changed.** The `Worms` module exists (server and client), as P2 planned, with these numbers and differences:

- **A worm** is a Model named by its participant id (`P_<UserId>`) in `Workspace.Ume.Worms`, streamed to everyone:
  an invisible ball `Root` 2.6 studs across (`ROOT_SIZE`) in the `Worm` collision group with friction 1
  (`ROOT_FRICTION`, so a resting worm holds on slopes up to about 45°), a `PlaneConstraint` to the arena's plane, an
  `AlignOrientation` "Upright" (facing ±X), a `LinearVelocity` "Walk" along X (off; WormControl turns it on), and the
  skin `Assets.Skins.Classic` welded on, massless. The `Classic` skin is a placeholder of 7 pink balls with eyes,
  only in the open place for now (`ReplicatedStorage.Ume.Assets.PlaceLoader.Default.Skins`).
- **Attributes:** `ParticipantId`, `UserId`, `DisplayName`, `Team`, `Color`, `IsBot`, `Health`, `MaxHealth`, `Alive`,
  `Ghost`, `Facing` (−1 or 1). Not yet: `HeldItem`, `AimAngle`, `Shiny`, `Vip`.
- **Health:** 200 (`MAX_HEALTH`). `Damage` rounds and stops at 0, but a worm at 0 stays in play: the Match module is
  meant to knock it out once the shot settles. `Heal` never goes above `MaxHealth`. `Knockback` is an impulse and
  needs the server to own the worm (WormControl takes it back first). The last attacker and item are kept for kill
  credit.
- **Each Heartbeat** (server): `I.Arena.GetHazard` along each worm's path since the last frame, in liquid-cell steps,
  so a fast worm can't skip through water; then fall damage on landing, 1 per stud above 10 (`SAFE_FALL_HEIGHT`),
  at most 40 (`MAX_FALL_DAMAGE`). A worm stands when the ground is within 0.4 studs under its ball (`GROUND_GAP`).
- **Knockout:** `Kill` sets `Health` 0, `Alive` false, `Ghost` true; the worm is anchored, joins the `Ghost` group,
  turns 0.6 see-through (`GHOST_TRANSPARENCY`) and floats 4 studs up over 1.5 s (`GHOST_RISE`, `GHOST_RISE_TIME`).
  Then the Topic `Worms_Died @ (participantId, cause, killerId?, itemId?)`, with cause `Water`, `Lava` or `Bounds`.
- **Client:** every worm wears a `WormTag` (name in team colour, health number and bar) 1.8 studs above its center
  (`TAG_OFFSET`), hidden on ghosts, with the turn marker when the worm has an `Active` attribute (the Match module
  will set it). A health drop pops a `DamagePop` ("-50") that rises 3 studs while it fades over 1 s. Not yet: the
  VIP gold name, the turn timer on the tag, the `DamageNumbers` setting (pops always show), hurt sounds, voice lines.
- **Not built:** animations (no `AnimationController`, as the skins have no animation rows yet), the held item's model,
  `IsSettled()`.
- **Admin command:** `/worms damage|heal <amount> [id]`, `/worms kill [id]`, `/worms push <x> <y> [id]` (a speed
  change in studs per second); each acts on the sender's worm by default.

**Where.** `Worms/Server.lua` (`I.Worms`: `Spawn`, `Damage`, `Heal`, `Knockback`, `Kill`, `SetOwner`, `Clear`, `Get`,
`GetAll`, `GetAlive`), `Scripts/Server/Bodies.lua`, `Health.lua`, `Hazards.lua`; `Worms/Client.lua` (`Get`,
`GetMine`) and `Scripts/Client/Tags.lua`; numbers in `Worms/Settings.lua`. The WormTag and DamagePop billboards are
moved at start from the gui loader's folder to `ReplicatedStorage.Ume.Worms`. No remotes: clients read attributes.

**How to check.** Start a Studio test (WormControl's `STUDIO_TEST_TURNS` shortcut gives each player a worm on the drop
zone). The worm's Z stays 0. `/worms damage 50` sets `Health` to 150 and pops "-50". `/arena carve` under the worm
over water drops it in: it turns into a ghost and the Output prints
`[WormControl::StudioTest]: Worms_Died P_<id> Water - -`. Both checked in Studio on 2026-11-03.

**For the wiki.** Match modules → Worms: `GetHazard` replaces `IsOut`, health 0 doesn't knock out by itself, the
attributes and features not built yet (above), the hazard check along the path. Match screen: the tag and damage
numbers as built. Roadmap: P2's Worms task, minus animations, held items and `IsSettled`. Still unsure: the tag's look
in game wasn't seen (Studio screenshots don't show billboards; its layout was checked by its numbers), lava wasn't
tested, and a ghost appears where it was knocked out, which can be underwater.
