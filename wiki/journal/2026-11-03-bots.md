**What changed.** Bots fill solo, public and tutorial matches. A bot thinks 1.5–3 s, picks a target and a weapon,
searches angles and powers with arc prediction until a shot lands near the target, then misses on purpose often
enough to hit at its rate: Easy 30 %, Medium 60 %, Hard 85 % (`HIT_RATE`). Loadouts: Easy Grenade and Shotgun; Medium
adds Dynamite; Hard Dynamite, Cluster Bomb, Uzi, Homing Missile, Baseball Bat. The tutorial bot aims beside the player
and never hits, and takes ×3 damage (`DAMAGE_TAKEN`), so two Bazooka hits (35 each) knock out its 200 health. The wiki
says "double damage"; ×2 would need three hits.

**Where.** `I.Bots.CreateParticipant(difficulty, number)`, `I.Bots.TakeTurn(id)`; `Bots/Settings.lua`.

**How to check.** A Studio match: bots take turns, fire, and sometimes hit.

**For the wiki.** Match modes & maps → solo difficulties; the tutorial bot's ×3.
