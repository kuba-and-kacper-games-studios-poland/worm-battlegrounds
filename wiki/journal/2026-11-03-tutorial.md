**What changed.** A new player's first join sends them to the tutorial match on Picnic Plains: 1v1 against the
tutorial bot (never hits them, falls to two Bazooka hits). Hints in order: walk, jump, aim and fire, the bot's turn,
"Two hits knock it out. Finish it!". At the end they get 100 coins and go back to the lobby (no rematch). There, a hint
and an arrow point to the tutorial crate (100 coins; always the Grenade and the Shotgun), then to the loadout; with
both equipped the tutorial is done. In Studio, which can't teleport, the lobby skips the match and starts at the crate.

**Where.** `I.Tutorial` (`CompleteMatch`, `IsMatchDone`, `IsDone`, `CanOpenCrate`); remote `Tutorial_Step("Equipped")`;
profile `Tutorial = { MatchDone, CrateOpened, Done }`; Replicator `Tutorial` (`CrateReady`, `EquipReady`, …).
`Tutorial/Settings.lua` (`MATCH_COINS`, `HINTS`).

**How to check.** Verified in the Studio lobby with a fresh profile: 100 coins arrive, the crate gives Grenade and
Shotgun once (a second open is refused), and equipping both ends the tutorial and hides the hints. The match part
needs a published place.

**For the wiki.** Lobby → First time playing. The bot takes ×3 damage, not the planned ×2 (see the bots entry).
