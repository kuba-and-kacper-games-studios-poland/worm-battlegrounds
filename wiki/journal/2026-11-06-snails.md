**What changed.** Every player and bot is now a **snail**, not a worm. The `Classic` skin is the owner's snail model
(a pink body with eye stalks and a tan spiral shell, two MeshParts named `Body` and `Shell`), in place of the
placeholder pink balls with eyes. It's scaled to 0.45 of the model as made: 4.1 studs tall, 3.6 long, its foot on the
bottom of the 2.6-stud ball, head forward. Skins other than Classic still fall back to it. Every word in the code says
snail now; the game's own name (Worm Battlegrounds, `WormBattlegrounds` theme and folders, `worm-battlegrounds`
repo folders) stays until the owner's planned rename to Snail Battlegrounds, done last.

**Where.**
- Modules: `Worms` → `Snails` (`I.Snails`, `Snails_Settings`, `Snails_Participant`, the `Snails_Died` Topic,
  `Workspace.Ume.Snails`, `ReplicatedStorage.Ume.Snails`, the admin command `snails`), `WormControl` →
  `SnailControl` (`I.SnailControl`, actions `SnailControl_Left`, `_Right`, `_Jump`, `_Stick`, admin command
  `snailcontrol`). `MATCH_ONLY` in `place_loader.lua` names them.
- Collision group `Worm` → `Snail` (Arena's `COLLISION_GROUPS`).
- The billboard gui `WormTag` → `SnailTag` (gui folder `SnailTag/`, `StarterGui.SnailTag` in the place).
- The lobby stage marker `LobbyStage.Markers.Worm` → `Snail` (Lobby's `SNAIL_MARKER`).
- The bot name `Wormzilla` → `Snailzilla` (Bots' `BOT_NAMES`).
- The skin: `ReplicatedStorage.Ume.Assets.PlaceLoader.Default.Skins.Classic` in the place; the old worm is kept in
  `ServerStorage.!dump.Classic_OldWorm`.
- Fitted to the taller snail (Snails' `Settings.lua`): `TAG_OFFSET` 1.8 → 2.4; `GEAR` Parachute 4.3 → 4.9 up,
  Jetpack (0.8 up, 1.3 behind) → (0.4 up, 2.4 behind), on the back of the shell.

**How to check.** Play in Studio: the lobby stage shows the snail; in a match every snail is side-on, shell behind,
and a jetpack sits on the shell's back. No saved data changed (no DataStore key named worms).

**For the wiki.** Every page that says worm says snail (The game, Lobby, Match screen, Controls, Arsenal, HUD
words), and the build pages name `Snails`, `SnailControl`, `SnailTag` and the `Snail` collision group. The Variety
grenade's minions were already called snails; the wiki may want to tell them apart from the players' snails. The
game's title stays until the rename.
