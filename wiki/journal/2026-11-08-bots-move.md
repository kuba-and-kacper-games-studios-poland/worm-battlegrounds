**What changed.** Bots no longer stand still. Each turn a bot thinks (1.2–2.2 s), heals with a Bandage or Medkit when
under 90 health, then moves with the players' walking speed, jump, ledge climb and stamina:

- **Close in** (Medium half the time, Hard 85%): walks up to a near target on about its level to swing the Baseball
  bat, or to place Dynamite, then runs to the side with more ground and flies up with its jetpack.
- **Reposition** (Medium and Hard): when its shot from where it stands would land more than 3 studs off, it walks or
  flies (jetpack, up to 14 studs) to the nearby spot whose shot lands nearest.
- Otherwise it wanders a few studs or hops (Easy bots always do this).

It never walks into the sea or down a drop that hurts, and only picks a gun when it has a clear line. The tutorial bot
still stands still. Loadouts: Easy Grenade, Shotgun, Jetpack; Medium Grenade, Shotgun, Dynamite, Jetpack, Bandage;
Hard Dynamite, Cluster bomb, Uzi, Homing missile, Baseball bat, Jetpack, Medkit.

**Where.** `TakeTurn`, `_heal`, `_closeIn`, `_reposition` in `Bots/Server.lua`; walking, jumps, the jetpack and safe
ground in `Bots/Scripts/Server/Move.lua` (`WalkTo`, `Hop`, `GetGround`, `GetRunway`). The numbers are in
`Bots/Settings.lua` (`LOADOUTS`, `THINK_TIME`, `HEAL_BELOW`, the Moving category: `CLOSE_IN_CHANCE`, `GOOD_MISS`,
`CLIMB_MAX`…).

**How to check.** Studio match (2026-11-08): bots moved 4–30 studs a turn, climbed up to 13 studs and hit the player.
Still unsure: a bot sometimes ran too short from its own Dynamite; the runway choice and jetpack that fix it weren't
retested on land.
