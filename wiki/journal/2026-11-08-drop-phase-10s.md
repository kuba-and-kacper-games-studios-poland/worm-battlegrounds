**What changed.** Players have 10 s to pick where their snail lands, down from 15 (it still ends early once everyone
placed), so matches start sooner.

**Where.** `DROP_SECONDS` in `Match/Settings.lua`.

**How to check.** In Studio, Play solo reached the first turn in about 14 s (it was about 19 s, 15 of them the drop).

**For the wiki.** Match modes & maps: the drop's timer.

**From.** The tester's notes ("loading too long to game"). See `2026-11-08-lobby-teleport-hardening` for what's still
unsure about loading.
