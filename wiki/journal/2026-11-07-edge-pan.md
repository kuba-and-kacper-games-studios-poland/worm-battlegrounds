**What changed.** The studio's wiki says players look around by dragging with the right mouse "or the screen edge".
Now holding the mouse at the screen's left or right edge (1.5 % of the screen's height from it) pans the view sideways
at 60 studs a second, while the window has focus and no right-drag is panning. The top and bottom edges don't pan:
Roblox's buttons sit along the top and the loadout bar along the bottom.

**Where.** `_getEdgePan` in `MatchCamera/Client.lua`; `EDGE_PAN_MARGIN` 0.015, `EDGE_PAN_SPEED` 60.

**How to check.** In a match on PC, move the mouse to the right edge: the view slides right until the arena's edge.
Not confirmed: in an automated Studio test the view didn't move with the mouse at the right edge, likely because the play window didn't have focus (the pan needs a focused window). Try it by hand.

**For the wiki.** Controls & platforms → Look around the map (PC): right-mouse drag, or the screen's side edges.
