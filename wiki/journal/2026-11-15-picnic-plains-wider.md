**What changed.** The owner's new Picnic Plains (dev place `Workspace.New`) replaced the old one, as built: three
islands at the waterline (x −190 to −93, −73 to 63, 100 to 190), each hollowed by a cave with stalactites and
stalagmites (52 parts, off the grid, no flags), four floating ledges (Y 27 to 57), a small pond on the middle island
(`Markers/Pond`, water), five block clouds in the sky (decor, not props). 1,098 land parts (was 728), 84 backdrop
parts, 49 props: trees, bushes, ferns, grass, flowers, two lilies on the pond, rocks, a log, and the studio's picnic
set (table, blanket, basket). Fixes: its Plastic parts became SmoothPlastic, 33 props stood on their ground (most by
under a stud; the lilies stay on the pond).

**Where.** In the dev place only: `Arenas.PicnicPlains` (slot 1); the one before is
`MapLab._Iterations.PicnicPlains_arena_4`. Its MapLab files are still the version before.

**How to check.** Studio playtest: loads with 1,098 terrain parts, 49 props, the sea and pond drawn, no errors. The
picnic table keeps its four legs after the arena moves it behind the snails.

**For the wiki.** Picnic Plains: its width, the caves, the pond, the picnic; now the widest map. The lobby card
picture still shows the old one.
