**What changed.** The owner's bot rewards: a solo win against Easy bots pays 20 diamonds, Medium 30, Hard 40 (were
10, 18, 30). Only a win pays, as before.

**Where.** MatchRewards' `SOLO_DIAMONDS` (what the server pays) and MatchBoard's `SOLO_WIN_DIAMONDS` (what the PLAY
SOLO cards show), kept equal. Both READMEs updated.

**How to check.** Lobby → PLAY SOLO: the three difficulty cards show 20, 30 and 40 as the win reward; win a solo match
and the payout matches.

**For the wiki.** Play solo / Diamonds: the reward per difficulty and a History line.
