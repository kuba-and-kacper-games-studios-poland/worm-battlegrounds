**What changed.** The Shop widget sells:

- **Diamond packs** (developer products): Handful 250, Pouch 500, Bag 1,000, Chest 1,500, Vault 2,500 diamonds.
- **Game passes:** VIP, Double Coins, Loadout slot 4, Loadout slot 5.

Prices are read live from Roblox. Every product and pass id is **0** (a placeholder) until the owner creates them; with
id 0 the shop shows "-" for the price. The currency bar's + and a SHOP top bar button (lobby only) open it.

**VIP:** a free skin crate once per UTC day (the skin crate card shows a badge and opens free), a gold name on the
worm's tag, `[VIP]` in front of chat messages, and the VIP skin when bought.

**Where.** `I.Shop` (`IsVip`, `CanClaimVipCrate`, `ClaimVipCrate`); profile `Shop.VipCrateDay`; Replicator `Shop`
(`VipCrateReady`); Player attribute `Vip` (copied to the worm). `Shop/Settings.lua` (`PACKS`, `PASSES`).

**How to check.** Needs real ids in a published place. In Studio the Shop opens with the packs and passes listed.

**For the wiki.** Currencies & crates → the shop; Lobby → top bar. The owner's step: create the 5 products and 4
passes, put their ids in `src/global_data/Marketplace/`.
