**What changed.** The match screen is the studio's `HUD` design, ported almost 1:1:

- **Top:** team panels left and right (new): each team's avatars outlined in its colour, darkened once knocked out,
  and its name ("Your team", "Red team"; in a free-for-all "You" or the snail's name). The player's team is on the
  left. Bots show the smiley icon (`BOT_AVATAR`). In the middle the turn timer as m:ss ("0:15"), the banner (YOUR TURN!,
  "Bax's turn", "You're next!") and the wind as an arrow and a signed number ("> +3").
- **Left:** the turn order (new), up to 6 (`TURN_LIST_ENTRIES`): the active snail highlighted, then who plays next,
  each with avatar and name in team colour.
- **Bottom:** six loadout slots (icon, name, "xN" uses; empty ones show "+"; a cooling slot is darkened with the
  turns left; the selected one has a white outline; key numbers on PC only), the stamina bar above them, the power
  slider at the bottom right ("NN%" on its handle, the angle or the homing hint above it).
- **Right:** the EMOTES button (opens the emote wheel as before).
- **Touch:** move buttons and a Jump button above them, and a FIRE button (new). On touch screens letting go of the
  drag keeps the aim and its power; FIRE shoots (it needs a target for the homing missile, power for thrown and
  arcing weapons). PC still fires on mouse release, pads with RT as before.
- **World labels:** the snail tag (name, health bar in team colour, number) and the damage number (yellow to red with
  a shadow) take the design's look. The crosshair is the studio's picture (`CROSSHAIR_IMAGE`, `rbxassetid://91912077604159`).
- Kept as they were: the kill feed (moved under the right team panel), the drop, waiting and knocked-out dialogs, the
  emote wheel.

The tutorial's aiming hint now says "Let go to fire, or press FIRE on a touch screen" (`HINTS.Fire`).

**How to check.** Studio solo match 2026-11-09: team panels, turn list, timer, wind, banner, stamina, slots and power
showed and updated through turns, a Bazooka shot fired; no errors. Not checked: phones (FIRE, move buttons), 4:3
screens, and the snail tags and damage numbers on screen (Studio's capture doesn't draw billboards).

**For the wiki.** Match screen: the new layout, team panels, turn list, wind display. Controls: FIRE on touch.
