**What changed.** The lobby's Crates panel opens crates one at a time or in 10-packs:

| Crate | Prices | Odds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Weapon | 100 coins, 10 for 900 | Uzi, Baseball Bat, Dynamite, Spartan Spear 14.75 %; Cluster Bomb, Spider Bomb, Minigun 10 %; Homing Missile, One Punch 5 %; Variety Grenade, Black Hole Grenade 0.5 % |
| Premium | 250 diamonds, 10 for 2,250 | Cluster, Spider, Minigun 26 %; Homing, One Punch 10 %; Variety, Black Hole 1 % |
| Utility | 100 coins (900) or 200 diamonds (1,800) | Coins: Parachute, Bandage 27.5 %; Jetpack, Speed 15 %; Medkit 10 %; Low Gravity 5 %. Diamonds: 15, 15, 20, 20, 20, 10 |
| Skin | 100 coins (900) or 50 diamonds (450) | Soldier, Pirate, Cowboy 20 %; Ninja, Knight, Clown 10 %; Devil 7 %; Robot 2 %; Skeleton 1 % |

Each card picks the currency it pays with. **Details** opens the odds window; opening shakes the crate, then shows
the item (or 10 cards) with its rarity and copies, and "Shiny!" on the 10th copy. Where the player's policy restricts
paid random items, diamond prices are hidden and refused (and the Premium crate with them); coin prices stay, since
coins are earned. The server checks at start that every crate's odds add up to 100.

**Where.** `I.Crates.Open(player, crateId, count, payWith)`, remote `Crates_Open` (a function), Topic
`Crates_Opened`; rows in `Crates/Data/Crates/`; `PAID_CURRENCIES = { "Diamonds" }`.

**How to check.** Measured in Studio: 20,000 weapon-crate rolls matched the table within half a percent. Opening with
too few coins is refused.

**For the wiki.** Currencies & crates (the table above, the policy rule). The reveal's burst effect and rarer
results' longer reveal aren't built. The "Skip" suggestion (crate-skip) is still open.
