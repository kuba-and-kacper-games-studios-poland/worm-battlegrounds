**What changed.** The `MatchHud` module drives the match screen: turn banner, timer, wind arrow, stamina bar, kill
feed (4 lines, 6 s each), loadout bar, touch walk and jump buttons, the waiting, drop and knocked-out blocks, and the
Results screen (placings, coins, XP, level up, stats, Rematch with votes and time left, Lobby).

- **Aiming:** drag back from the worm (mouse or touch): direction is the angle, length the power (24 to 260 px).
  Gamepad: right stick aims, hold RT to sweep the power. Right mouse, Esc or B cancels. Feet weapons fire on the press;
  homing weapons take a target tap first; a press during the flight detonates a Black Hole Grenade.
- **Slots:** keys 1–6 pick the Bazooka and slots 1–5; LB and RB step through them. A utility is used as soon as it's
  picked on the player's turn.
- **Top bar:** Leave (asks first) and Music (mutes and restores). The backpack button and the Console module's
  backpack bindings are gone.
- **Settings:** a Gameplay section with **Damage numbers** and **Camera follows the shot** (both on).
- **Worm tags:** a VIP player's name is gold (`VIP_NAME_COLOR` 255, 206, 46) and the tag shows the level ("Lv 12").
- **ScreenScale** sizes every game panel to the screen (height / 1080, ×1.3 on phones under 500 px).

**Where.** `MatchHud/Scripts/Client/*`; `MatchHud/Settings.lua` (`MIN_DRAG`, `MAX_DRAG`, `SLOT_KEYS`, `FEED_*`).
Settings keys `DamageNumbers`, `CameraFollowsShot`.

**How to check.** In a Studio match the HUD shows, keys 1–6 select, and Results open at the end.

**For the wiki.** Match screen and Controls & platforms (the controls above). Not checked with a real mouse drag:
