**What changed.** The melee weapon "Baseball bat" is renamed "Golf club", with the studio's golf club model (the
`gfclub` mesh) and its own icon. Its numbers are unchanged: 60 damage, VeryHigh knockback, reach 4, 3 uses. Its item
id stays `BaseballBat`, so players who own it keep it.

**Where.** `Name` and `IconId` / `Icon` (rbxassetid://74452433921921) in both rows; the model is
`Workspace.Ume.ReplicatedStorage_Assets.Arsenal.Models.BaseballBat` in the place (the old bat's body is in
`ServerStorage.!dump`). Its swing sound is still `Fire_BaseballBat`.

**How to check.** The loadout, crates and HUD show "Golf club" with the golf picture; a snail holds a golf club.
