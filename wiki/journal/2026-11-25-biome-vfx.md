**What changed.** The studio's biome effects (Workspace `BiomeVfx`: `Lava` and `Picnic`, placed on its own reference
copies of the maps) are in the maps (the owner: "add the vfx of lava to Volcano isle, RockslideRidge, and picnic to
PicnicPlain"). Each of those map templates has a `Vfx` folder of invisible parts (no collision, touch or queries)
holding the emitters, which the Arena loader clones and moves with the map like its other folders:

- Volcano Isle and Rockslide Ridge: `fire` (flames, wisps and sparks) as a flat sheet just over the lava sea's surface
  (6.5 and 15 studs up), and `Smoke` (fog, dust and specks) from there up 0.65 of the land's height, both as wide and
  as deep as the land.
- Picnic Plains: three `Clouds` near the top of the play area (at the reference's places along the map, 26 and 8 studs
  under the bounds' top), `Sun Rays` across the map 16 under the top, and falling `Leafs` under the three oaks'
  canopies.

**Where.** In the place only: `Workspace.Ume.ServerStorage_Assets.Arenas.<Map>.Vfx`. The emitters' settings are the
studio's, unchanged. `Workspace.BiomeVfx` (the source) is still in the Workspace.

**How to check.** Play a match on each map: embers over the lava and smoke in the air; on Picnic Plains leaves falling
from the oaks, clouds and rays above. Checked in Studio on Picnic Plains (the leaves) and in edit mode on Rockslide
Ridge. The place isn't saved yet.

**For the wiki.** Each map's page: a line on its atmosphere effects.
