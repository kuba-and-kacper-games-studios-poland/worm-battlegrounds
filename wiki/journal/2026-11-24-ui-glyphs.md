**What changed.** The lobby's icons are new white glyphs with a thick ink outline, in the `LobbyRework` design's look:
deathmatch (a crosshair), duo and trio (two and three people), play, local (a gamepad), custom (sliders), the free
weapons gift, style (a paint brush), weapons (a rocket) and skins (a shell). They replace the 2026-11-17 mode icons on
the public cards, PLAY SOLO (play, for solo and party), LOCAL and CUSTOM, and the drawn pictures on the STYLE, WEAPONS,
SKINS and FREE WEAPONS tiles (0.54 of the tile, centred on its slanted body at 0.545, 0.39: the owner, "make sure the
icons are centered"). Quads keeps its old icon. Weapon and item pictures keep their look.

**Where.** `art/ui_glyphs` (sources and the upload table: decal and image ids, uploaded to The Snail Studio group,
all approved). MatchBoard's `MODE_ICONS`; the MatchBoard gui's LOCAL, CUSTOM and `S_SoloIcon`; the Lobby gui's menu
tile `Icon`s; the FreeCrate gui's `S_TileIcon`.

**How to check.** The lobby's cards, buttons and menu tiles all show the white glyphs.

**For the wiki.** Lobby and Match modes & maps: new icon pictures.

Supersedes 2026-11-17-mode-icons for every icon but Quads (and Teams and Tutorial, which have no place in the lobby).
