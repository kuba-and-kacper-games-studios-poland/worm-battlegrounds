Crate Opening

Draft 2 of the unboxing: buying drops real 3D crates onto the screen; tap them to open (hatches first, then the walls), and the CS:GO-style reel follows. A legendary brings a golden crate to tap open again. Try x1, x3 and x10, and "Next drop: Legendary" for the golden crate. Sound starts with your first click.

Sounds

A hybrid, as asked: the reel's ticks, the lock and the latch are crisp new synthesis in the CS:GO manner; everything musical (reveals, fanfares, the legendary calls) plays on the game's own classic Roblox soundscape, The Great Strategy and Wind of Fjords samples and Roblox's 2009 sounds. Nothing is uploaded yet.

Icons

Drawn crates in the game's icon style: thick ink outline, a lit top and a shaded side, so each sits on its pedestal with depth. Each crate's colour is its theme on the GACHA screen. The lobby's empty STYLE, WEAPONS and SKINS buttons get pictures too. Skin portraits and the One Punch, Baseball bat and Parachute pictures are stand-ins for this preview only, since those items still use the placeholder.
